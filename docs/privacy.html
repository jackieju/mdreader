Privacy Policy

MD Reader (Markdown Reader - MD View)

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Overview

MD Reader is a Markdown file viewer for iOS and macOS. The app is designed to work entirely on your device.

Data Collection

MD Reader does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no network communication of your content.

File Access

MD Reader reads Markdown (.md) files that you explicitly choose to open. These files are read locally on your device solely to render them for viewing. File contents are never uploaded, copied to any server, or shared with the developer or any third party.

Third Parties

MD Reader does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics services, or advertising networks.

Children's Privacy

MD Reader does not collect any information from anyone, including children.
